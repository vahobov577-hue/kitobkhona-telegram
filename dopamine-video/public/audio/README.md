Drop your own track here as `music.mp3` — it is picked up automatically instead of the
generated `music.wav`. The required dramaturgy is kept with a volume curve:
one bar of silence at 0:39 (frames 1170–1242) and full silence 2:00–2:10 (frames 3540–3900).

`music.wav` and `sfx/*.wav` are generated by `npm run audio`.
